
Moondru Mugam (1982) –
Characters: Alex Pandian (an honest cop), Arun, and John (his twin sons).
* An upright police officer, Alex Pandian, mercilessly cracks down on a ruthless crime lord named Egambaram.
* Enraged by his interference, the villain brutally murders Alex Pandian and separates his family.
* Years later, his twin sons, who grew up in completely different environments, accidentally cross paths.
* Discovering their true lineage, they join forces to exact perfect revenge on their father's killer.

Billa (1980) – 
Characters: Billa (a powerful mafia don) and Rajappa (an innocent village lookalike).
* Billa is a ruthless, high-profile international underworld kingpin hunted by the police.
* During a chase, a police inspector accidentally kills Billa but hides his death from the public.
* To infiltrate the crime syndicate, the inspector trains a simple, fun-loving village man named Rajappa.
* Rajappa successfully acts as Billa, but things go awry when the inspector dies, leaving Rajappa trapped in a dangerous criminal web.

Netrikkan (1981) –  
Characters: Chakravarthi (a womanizing father) and Santhosh (his righteous son).
* Chakravarthi is a wealthy, middle-aged businessman who uses his power to exploit young women.
* His son, Santhosh, stands up for deep moral values and strongly opposes his father’s predatory actions.
* When the father crosses a line by ruining the life of an innocent girl, the son decides to teach him a lesson.
* Santhosh ingeniously plots against his own father to systematically bring down his pride and force him to reform.

Enthiran (2010) – 
Characters: Dr. Vaseegaran (a brilliant scientist) and Chitti (an advanced humanoid robot).
* Dr. Vaseegaran spends years building Chitti, a highly sophisticated robot designed to assist the Indian military.
* To help Chitti fit into human society, the scientist programs complex emotional capabilities into him.
* Chaos erupts when Chitti falls deeply in love with the scientist's fiancée, Sana.
* An evil rival scientist later manipulates Chitti's code, turning him into a destructive monster threatening the world.

Kochadaiiyaan 
Characters: Kochadaiiyaan (a legendary commander), Rana, and Sena (his sons).
* Commander Kochadaiiyaan is a fiercely loyal military general who gets wrongfully framed for treason and executed.
* His young son, Rana, flees the kingdom but harbors a deep flame of vengeance inside him.
* Years later, Rana returns in disguise, rises rapidly through the military ranks, and wins the king's trust.
* Alongside his brother, he systematically exposes the royal court's conspiracies to restore his father's ruined honor.



Thillu Mullu (1981)
The Lie: Chandran fakes an imaginary, traditional twin brother named Indran.
* Chandran lands a great job by pretending to be a strictly traditional, sports-hating, orthodox young man.
* He gets caught watching a football match by his boss, who strongly values discipline and honesty.
* To save his job, Chandran invents a wild lie that the person at the stadium was his rebellious, clean-shaven twin brother, Indran.
* He is forced to constantly switch characters, altering his mustache and voice, leading to a hilarious comedy of errors.

Chandramukhi (2005)
The Lie: Dr. Saravanan briefly acts as King Vettaiyan to trick a deeply disturbed mind.
* A psychologist named Saravanan visits an ancestral mansion to help his friend's wife, Ganga, who suffers from split personality disorder.
* Ganga believes she is Chandramukhi, an ancient court dancer obsessed with killing the cruel King Vettaiyan.
* To permanently cure her mind without causing harm, Saravanan decides to orchestrate a massive psychological trick.
* During the film's climax, he completely transforms into the persona of King Vettaiyan to satisfy her spirit's thirst for revenge.

Baasha (1995)
The Lie: Manikkam hides his true legacy as an underworld crime don under a peaceful facade.
* Manikkam is a humble, peace-loving auto-rickshaw driver who goes to great lengths to avoid any form of violence.
* He works hard to support his siblings and keeps a very low profile in society.
* However, he hides a massive secret about his dark past in Mumbai.
* He was once Baasha, a dangerous and legendary underworld mafia don who ruled the city's crime syndicates.
* When a cruel local gangster threatens his family, Manikkam is forced to drop his peaceful facade and unleash his fearsome old identity.
